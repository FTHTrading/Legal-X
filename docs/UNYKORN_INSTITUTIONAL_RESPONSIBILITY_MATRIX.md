# UnyKorn & Legal-X Institutional Shared Responsibility Matrix & Regulatory Boundary

## 1. Executive Summary: Technology Infrastructure Identity

UnyKorn and Legal-X provide **configurable software infrastructure** for workflow management, cryptographic record integrity, data aggregation, smart-contract orchestration, audit logging, and authorized third-party API integration.

---

## 2. Permanent Legal & Operational Boundary

### What UnyKorn & Legal-X Is:
- A verifiable software orchestration layer.
- An immutable evidence and document-custody lineage architecture (FRE 902 / BLAKE2b).
- A workflow state machine for matter management, estate preservation, and corporate approvals.
- An API integration adapter for client-authorized qualified custodians, banks, and transfer agents.

### What UnyKorn & Legal-X Is NOT:
Unless expressly agreed in a separate written agreement with an appropriately licensed affiliate or third-party provider, UnyKorn and Legal-X **do not** act as:
- A Broker-Dealer or Alternative Trading System (ATS).
- A Securities Exchange or Clearing Agency.
- An SEC-Registered Transfer Agent.
- A Qualified Custodian or Trust Bank.
- A Money Transmitter or Depository Institution.
- A Legal Counsel, Law Firm, Tax Advisor, or Regulatory Filing Agent.

### Customer Responsibility:
Customers, issuers, and financial institutions remain exclusively responsible for confirming that their intended use, connected counterparties, asset structures, disclosures, supervisory procedures, records, permissions, and regulatory obligations are appropriate for their jurisdiction and business model.

## 4. Operational Release Standards & Claim Classifications

Every feature, module, and integration claim within UnyKorn and Legal-X must carry an unambiguous status classification:

1. **Live:** Accessible, enabled, and operational in production software.
2. **Live with Third-Party Dependency:** Operational only after client executes separate account agreement with authorized third-party provider (e.g., BitGo Enterprise).
3. **Pilot:** Limited authorized users, controlled scope, not generally available.
4. **Sandbox:** Demonstration or simulation mode; no real transactions or regulatory reliance.
5. **Design / Roadmap:** Architectural specification or interface model; capability is planned.
6. **Customer-Configured:** Operational only after customer establishes internal policies, legal counsel review, and third-party vendor credentials.

## 5. Institutional Diligence & Data Room Framework

For institutional counterparties, prospective enterprise partners, and regulatory compliance reviews, the following documentation package is maintained:

1. **Entity & Authority Proof:** Wyoming formation documentation, Good Standing Certificate, EIN documentation (under NDA), and authorized corporate signatory resolutions.
2. **Vendor & Integration Matrix:** Complete inventory of third-party API adapters, environment scopes, data flows, and security boundaries.
3. **Information Security Evidence:** Zero-knowledge client-side encryption model (AES-256-GCM), server-side secret isolation, static IP egress restrictions, and business continuity runbooks.
4. **SEC Rule 17a-4 / Books & Records Mapping:** Immutable WORM log retention architecture, indexing specifications, supervisory review access queues, and audit export rails.
5. **Model Risk & Calculation Governance:** Calculation workspaces (e.g. net-capital data staging, customer reserve formulas) with versioned methodologies, exception flags, and mandatory supervisory review gates.
