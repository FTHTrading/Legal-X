The Protocol Process

More than storage. A Legal‑X legacy system.

Legacy Vault helps families and trusted professionals organize sensitive records, preserve digital-asset instructions, define trusted release rules, and create verifiable proof of important documents and events.

01

Ingest & Client-Side Encrypt

Upload estate documents, deeds, digital keys, passwords, and instructions. Encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM before touching the network.

02

Designate Fiduciary Guardians

Select family members, trusted attorneys, or corporate fiduciaries. Set quorum thresholds (e.g. 3-of-5 required for key reconstruction).

03

Anchor Verifiable Evidence

Legal-X generates deterministic proof digests anchored on-chain for self-authenticating validity under Federal Rule of Evidence 902.

04

Continuous Family Continuity

When release triggers occur, guardians verify identity, reach threshold quorum, and securely receive instructions without probate delays.
